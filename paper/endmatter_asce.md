# Data Availability Statement {.unnumbered}

Some or all data, models, or code generated or used during the study are available in a repository online in accordance with funder data retention policies. The source code (the pyRevit extension and the Python analysis package), the experiment scripts, the result tables, the figures, the supplemental video and the expert rating instrument are available at <https://github.com/Darkiii-2514/DT-Clash-Detection-Paper>.

Some or all data, models, or code used during the study were provided by a third party: the two case-study models, the Snowdon Towers sample (structural model with its architectural, HVAC, plumbing, electrical and site links) and BIM_Projekt_Golden_Nugget (Architektur_und_Ingenieurbau model with its Gebaeudetechnik link), which are sample files distributed by Autodesk, Inc. with Autodesk Revit 2027. Direct requests for these materials may be made to the provider as indicated in the Acknowledgments.

Some or all data, models, or code generated or used during the study are proprietary or confidential in nature and may only be provided with restrictions: the two case-study models are distributed under the Autodesk Revit 2027 software licence and are therefore not redistributed with this study. They are installed with Revit 2027, and the released extraction scripts regenerate the model exports used in the analysis.
