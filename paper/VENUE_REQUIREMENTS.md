# Automation in Construction — submission requirements (read from the official guide, 2026-10-10)

Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/automation-in-construction/publish/guide-for-authors

- Article type: Original paper. Single-anonymized review.
- **Abstract ≤ 150 words**, stand-alone, no references, no uncommon abbreviations.
- **Keywords: 1–7**, English, avoid multi-word "and/of" phrases.
- **Highlights: 3–5 bullets, each ≤ 85 characters incl. spaces**, separate editable file with "highlights" in the file name.
- Graphical abstract: encouraged (not required).
- Numbered sections 1, 1.1, 1.1.1; abstract not numbered.
- **References: numbered in square brackets in order of appearance**, e.g. "as demonstrated [3,6]"; > 6 authors → first 6 then "et al."; shortened last page (51-9).
- Math: editable text, display equations numbered consecutively; variables italic; use solidus for small fractions.
- Tables: editable text (not images).
- Required statements: **CRediT author contributions**, **Declaration of competing interests**, **Data statement (availability)**, **Declaration of generative AI use** ("During the preparation of this work the author(s) used [TOOL] in order to [REASON]. After using this tool/service, the author(s) reviewed and edited the content as needed and take(s) full responsibility for the content of the published article.") — AI-generated images must also be disclosed in captions.
